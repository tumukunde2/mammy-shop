MAMMY SHOP is a Rwandan-based e-commerce store that aims to provide quality, reliable and consistent services in the retail and distribution of essential products used in daily life. Based on the principles of honesty, quality and reliability, MAMMY SHOP is committed to helping the Rwandan community find quality products at a price that is affordable for everyone.

MAMMY SHOP yashinzwe mu rwego rwo gukemura ibibazo by’abaturage bahura n’imbogamizi zo kubona ibicuruzwa byiza hafi yabo, ku giciro cyoroheje kandi byujuje ubuziranenge. Twabonye ko hari icyuho kinini mu gucuruza ibicuruzwa by’ibanze mu buryo bwizewe, bituma dufata icyemezo cyo gushyiraho iduka rihora ku isonga mu gutanga ibisubizo byiza kandi byihuse ku bakiriya bacu.
